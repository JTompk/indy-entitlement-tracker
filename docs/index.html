About data coverage

Weekly agenda records (rezonings, variances, plats, tax incentives) begin mid-2025, when this tracker started reading DMD and IHPC agendas. Years before that are not a complete record of filings — a quiet map in 2023 means we weren't watching yet, not that nothing happened.

City case layers (approvals, variances, non-conforming uses) come from MapIndy's GIS and reach back decades, but reflect the city's own record-keeping, including its gaps and occasional data-entry errors.

Coverage grows every week from here. Spot something wrong? Tell us.

Solid dots are petitions compiled weekly from public DMD board agendas. Ring markers are case layers served by the city's own MapIndy GIS. Locations may be approximate; verify against the linked source.
