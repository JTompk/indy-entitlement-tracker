How the corridors are drawn

TOD overlay polygons come from the Marion County Land Use Plan's Transit-Oriented Development overlay, the same layer used in our Zoning Gap work.

Trail walksheds are a quarter mile (1,320 ft) drawn around every built trail in the city's DPW trails inventory — the Monon, Fall Creek, Pleasant Run, the Cultural Trail, and hundreds of smaller segments. Planned trails and segments the inventory labels as plain sidewalks are excluded.

Weekly agenda records begin mid-2025, when the tracker started reading DMD and IHPC agendas. A quiet corridor before that means we weren't watching yet, not that nothing happened.
